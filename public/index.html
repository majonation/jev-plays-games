THE FLIGHT PLAN

A tiny loop.
A smarter flight.

01

Observe the sky

Bird position, velocity, pipe gaps, and predicted trajectories become structured input for Jev.

02

Make a decision

OpenRouter sends the state to Jev’s Decisions API. Jev returns a flap or coast choice, with probabilities when available. The game applies its choice.

03

Let it fly

The sky moves continuously while Jev replies in the background. Each reply is used once at the next control step. The optional collision guard checks the current position and can cover delayed replies. Each guard intervention is labeled separately from Jev's decisions. Crashes are still possible.

Connect your pilot

Add your OpenRouter key to .env, then restart the server. 

OPENROUTER_API_KEY=your_key_here

In Your turn mode, press Space or tap the game to flap. Press P to pause.
